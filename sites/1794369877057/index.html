TIME, INTENT, AND
UNHURRIED HEAT.

Modern high-volume coffee rushes the bean through infernos in under six minutes, scalding delicate cell structures. We favor a quiet, lengthened RoR (Rate of Rise) curve that respects each origin's altitude density.

Each batch is hand-logged with acoustic monitors that detect the exact second of first crack, maximizing internal sugars while preserving shimmering citric brilliance.
